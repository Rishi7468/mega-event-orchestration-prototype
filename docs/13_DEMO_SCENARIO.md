# 13 — Demo Scenario

## Demo objective

Show that the product solves a visitor problem while simultaneously orchestrating destination capacity.

## Target duration

2–3 minutes.

## Story structure

```text
Visitor intent
    ↓
Intelligent plan
    ↓
Live event changes
    ↓
Prediction
    ↓
Visitor re-optimization
    ↓
Operator coordination
    ↓
Measurable outcome
```

## Scene 1 — Visitor planning

Open mobile visitor experience.

Enter:

- event
- 2 visitors
- 2 nights
- ₹2,000 budget
- desired arrival

Click:

`Build My Plan`

## Scene 2 — Accommodation intelligence

Show:

Central:

- expensive
- limited availability
- high pressure

North:

- good availability
- direct shuttle
- moderate crowd

East:

- cheap
- farther away

System recommends North.

Say:

> The system is not just finding a hotel. It is considering destination capacity, transport availability, crowd pressure, and the visitor's preferences together.

## Scene 3 — Journey

Generate:

Hotel → Transit Hub → Shuttle → Gate B → Venue.

Show:

`42 min`

`Moderate congestion`

## Scene 4 — Trigger change

Click:

`Simulate Demand Spike`

A sudden event demand change occurs.

## Scene 5 — Prediction

Show:

> Gate A and the Central Zone are projected to become congested.

## Scene 6 — Visitor re-optimization

Mobile alert:

> **Your plan can be improved**

> Gate B is currently less crowded.

> Estimated time saved: 11 min.

Click:

`Update My Route`

## Scene 7 — Operator view

Switch to desktop.

Show:

Central pressure:

`91%`

North pressure:

`48%`

Transport utilization:

`86%`

The operator sees:

> Redistribution opportunity detected.

## Scene 8 — Operator action

Recommendation:

> Shift arrivals toward North and Gate B.

Click:

`Approve Response`

## Scene 9 — Outcome

Show:

```text
Central pressure       91% → 72%
North pressure         48% → 59%
Transport utilization  86% → 68%
Visitor travel         42m → 31m
```

## Final message

Use a line similar to:

> **We don't just monitor crowds or find hotels. We orchestrate where demand goes — helping visitors make better decisions while helping destinations use their capacity more intelligently.**

## Demo discipline

Do not:

- open every feature,
- explain implementation,
- spend time on authentication,
- show empty screens,
- claim real-world deployment,
- call simulated numbers real measurements.

## Recording preparation

Before recording:

- reset simulation
- confirm local video works
- confirm map works
- confirm all recommendation buttons work
- remove development warnings
- use believable event names
- keep cursor movement deliberate
- rehearse the exact sequence

## Judge takeaway

The judge should remember:

**Visitor benefit + destination benefit from one intelligence layer.**
